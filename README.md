# Loan Business Profitability Analysis

An Excel-based analysis of a **500-loan portfolio** (Car, Bike, Laptop, Mobile Phone) that identifies where the business is losing money and what management can do about it.

![Loan Portfolio Dashboard](dashboard_screenshot.png)

## Overview

The portfolio is currently **loss-making**: Net Bank Profit of approximately **−₹36.05 lakh** on a **−2.83%** margin. The analysis uses PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPIs, slicers, and a dashboard to trace this loss back to its root causes.

## Key KPIs

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Loans | 500 |
| Total Loan Principal | ₹12.74 Cr |
| Total Revenue | ₹3.32 Cr |
| Total Cost | ₹3.68 Cr |
| Net Bank Profit | −₹36.05 Lakh |
| Profit Margin | −2.83% |

## Key Findings

1. **Car loans are the main problem** — approximately −₹37.41 lakh loss, enough to wipe out gains from every other product.
2. **Low-rate loans underperform** — the Low Rate segment loses ~₹26.69 lakh.
3. **Pricing risk is concentrated in Car loans** — Car + Low Rate and Car + Medium Rate drive most of the loss.
4. **Long-term Car loans are the worst combination** — Car + Long Term loses ~₹33.82 lakh.
5. **Every region is loss-making** (East largest at −₹11.71 lakh), so the fix isn't geographic — it's product economics.

## Recommended Actions

1. **Fix Car-loan economics** — review rate, tenure, size, and expected return independently.
2. **Reprice low-rate lending** — set a pricing floor based on funding cost and expected loss.
3. **Control long-term Car exposure** — shorter max tenure or added pricing for longer terms.
4. **Control large exposures** — stricter approval for big loans.
5. **Protect profitable products** — Laptop and Bike are currently profitable; grow them carefully.
6. **Review East region** — but only after checking its product/rate/tenure mix.

## Tools Used

Microsoft Excel — Tables, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, Calculated Fields, Conditional Formatting, Dashboard Design.
